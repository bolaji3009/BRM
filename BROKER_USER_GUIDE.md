### Key Portal Modules & How-To Guide

#### 1. SBU Profile & Accreditation Details
* **Overview:** View your verified corporate credentials, accreditation status, assigned SBU category, and NUBAN bank payout details.
* **Key Fields:**
  * **Broker Code:** Unique system identifier (e.g. `HMO-BRK-1001`).
  * **Partnership Tier:** `Elite Partner`, `Corporate Broker`, or `Retail Partner`.
  * **Tax Identification Number (TIN):** Verified FIRS tax profile.
  * **Payout Bank Details:** Verified corporate account number and bank name for NUBAN settlements.

---

#### 2. Corporate SBU Bids & Quote Generation
* **Applicability:** Corporate SBU Brokers.
* **Features:**
  1. **Active Corporate Bids Log:** Track all corporate bids submitted on behalf of client accounts (e.g. *Dangote Foods*, *Flutterwave Tech*), including workforce headcounts, annual premiums, and pipeline stages.
  2. **Corporate Proposal Generator:** Calculate real-time employee healthcare premiums with dynamic volume discounts:
     * **100+ employees:** 15% volume discount.
     * **50–99 employees:** 10% volume discount.
  3. **Proposal PDF Download:** Generate formal co-branded PDF quote proposals instantly to present to corporate client HR teams.

---

#### 3. Retail SBU Referral Code & MApp Sharing
* **Applicability:** Retail SBU Partners.
* **Features:**
  1. **Unique Referral Code:** Access your assigned alphanumeric code (e.g., `MAPP-BABAJIDE-01`).
  2. **Deep Link Sharing:** Click **Copy Referral Link** to copy your direct MApp enrolment URL (`https://mapp.miterahealth.com/enrol?ref=MAPP-BABAJIDE-01`).
  3. **Attribution Tracking:** Track total enrolments, qualifying gross premium, and earned retail commissions (3% split).
  4. **Anti-Self-Referral Warning:** Enrolments matching the broker's own identity, payment account, or mobile device hash are automatically held for compliance audit.

---

#### 4. Earned Commission Wallet & Tax Summary
* **Overview:** Track gross earned commissions, statutory FIRS Withholding Tax (WHT) deductions, and net wallet balances.
* **Financial Calculations:**
  * **Corporate SBU:** Gross Premium × Negotiated Rate % (e.g., 10%) - 5% Corporate WHT = Net Payout.
  * **Retail SBU:** Gross Premium × 3% Retail Rate - 10% Individual WHT = Net Payout.
* **Statement Export:** Download official PDF Commission Statements & WHT Deduction Certificates for tax reporting.

---

#### 5. Raising a Financial Dispute or Adjustment Claim
* **Overview:** If you identify a discrepancy in enrolment attribution, rate application, or commission payout, file a formal dispute directly from your portal header.
* **Step-by-Step Filing Procedure:**
  1. Click the red **Raise Adjustment / Dispute** button in the header.
  2. Select the **Dispute Category**:
     * *Missing Enrolment Attribution (MApp / Corporate)*
     * *Incorrect Agreement Rate Applied*
     * *FIRS WHT Calculation Discrepancy*
     * *Delayed Settlement Payout*
  3. Enter the **Client Name or Referral Code Reference**.
  4. Enter the **Claimed Financial Adjustment Amount (₦)**.
  5. Provide a detailed **Explanation & Evidence Summary**.
  6. Click **Submit Dispute & Log Adjustment**.
* **What Happens Next?** Your claim is assigned a tracking number (e.g. `DSP-2026-002`) and routed to the HMO Backend Master Resolution Queue. When resolved, an append-only credit entry is issued in your wallet without altering past receipts.
